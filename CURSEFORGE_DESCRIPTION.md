# Apricity Quests

**A refreshed quest-book interface for FTB Quests.** Apricity Quests presents your existing quest chapters, maps, objectives, and rewards in a clean, animated interface built on ApricityUI—without replacing your quest-pack content.

## Features

- **Explore chapters and quest maps:** Browse chapter groups, collapse the chapter list, and pan around large quest maps.
- **Find quests faster:** Search the current chapter or all chapters, with controls for searching chapter names, quests and descriptions, tasks, and rewards.
- **Choose a look:** Pick from six accent themes: green, yellow, blue, cyan, purple, and pink.
- **See quest details at a glance:** View status, description, progress, objectives, and rewards in a focused details panel.
- **Use familiar FTB Quests actions:** Complete objectives and claim rewards through the existing FTB Quests systems. Players with edit permission can open the FTB Quests editor from the interface.
- **Keep your place:** Map zoom and center are saved per chapter.
- **Customize the interface:** The default `questbook-v8.html` page and `questbook-v8.css` stylesheet are installed in `<Minecraft instance>/apricity/questui/`. Player edits are preserved when the mod updates its defaults.

Apricity Quests changes how the quest book is presented; it does not add quests or alter quest-pack progression. The bundled interface text is currently in Simplified Chinese; quest titles and descriptions are provided by your quest pack.

## Requirements

- Minecraft: **Java Edition 1.20.1**
- Mod loader: **Minecraft Forge 47.x**
- **FTB Quests** 2001.4.22 or newer
- **ApricityUI** 1.2.5 or newer

FTB Quests and ApricityUI are required dependencies and are installed separately. This release is for Forge; it is not a Fabric or NeoForge build.

## 中文简介

**Apricity Quests 为 FTB Quests 带来焕新的任务手册界面。** 它使用 ApricityUI 展示现有任务章节、任务地图、目标与奖励，不会替换或新增整合包中的任务内容。

### 功能特色

- **浏览章节与任务地图：** 支持章节组折叠、章节栏收起，以及拖动查看大型任务地图。
- **快速搜索：** 可搜索当前章节或全部章节，并分别控制章节名称、任务名称与说明、目标及奖励的搜索范围。
- **六种主题色：** 绿色、黄色、蓝色、青色、紫色和粉色。
- **清晰查看任务详情：** 集中显示任务状态、说明、进度、目标和奖励。
- **沿用 FTB Quests 原有交互：** 目标操作和奖励领取仍由 FTB Quests 处理；拥有编辑权限的玩家可从界面打开 FTB Quests 编辑器。
- **记住地图位置：** 每个章节的地图缩放与中心位置会分别保存。
- **支持自定义界面：** 默认页面 `questbook-v8.html` 和样式表 `questbook-v8.css` 安装在 `<Minecraft 实例目录>/apricity/questui/`。更新默认文件时会保留玩家自定义的修改。

Apricity Quests 只改变任务手册的呈现方式，不会添加任务或改写整合包的任务进度。当前内置界面文字为简体中文；任务名称与说明由整合包提供。

### 运行依赖

- Minecraft Java 版 **1.20.1**
- **Minecraft Forge 47.x**
- **FTB Quests** 2001.4.22 或更新版本
- **ApricityUI** 1.2.5 或更新版本

FTB Quests 和 ApricityUI 是必需依赖，需要单独安装。本版本适用于 Forge，不适用于 Fabric 或 NeoForge。
